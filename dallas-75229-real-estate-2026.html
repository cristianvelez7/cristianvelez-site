Dallas 75229 real estate: what buyers need to know about this market in 2026

By Cristian Velez · Licensed Realtor, Corey Simpson & Associates · Principal, Elite Offer Group

The 75229 ZIP code in Dallas is one of the most misunderstood markets in the city. It sits at the edge of Preston Hollow, one of Dallas's most established luxury neighborhoods, and shares boundaries, school zones, and street character with some of the most sought-after residential corridors in North Texas. But 75229 is its own market with its own pricing dynamics, its own buyer profile, and its own logic — and buyers who approach it with generic luxury market assumptions often make costly mistakes.

Here is what you actually need to know about buying or selling in Dallas 75229 in 2026.

What 75229 actually is

Preston Hollow spans multiple ZIP codes including 75220, 75225, 75229, and 75230, bordered generally by Royal Lane, Midway Road, Northwest Highway, and Hillcrest Road. The 75229 ZIP covers the eastern and central sections of this corridor, including the Midway Hollow, Midway Hills, and Little Preston Hollow neighborhoods. These are established areas with mature tree canopy, generous lot sizes, and a mix of classic mid-century architecture alongside newer custom construction.

Preston Hollow is defined by some of the largest residential lots in Dallas, averaging about 1.5 acres, with homes typically around 5,500 square feet. The 75229 section of this corridor offers buyers access to that land and scale at price points that are often more accessible than the deeper Preston Hollow estate sections in 75225 and 75230.

The market in 2026

The median sale price for homes in Preston Hollow over the last 12 months is $1,295,000, up 8 percent from the previous 12 months. Homes in Preston Hollow sell after 52 days on the market on average. That is a strong performance in the context of the broader DFW market softening, and it reflects the sustained demand from buyers who want established Dallas neighborhoods with genuine lot size and proximity to the city's top private schools, medical districts, and employment centers.

The 75229 market specifically spans a wide price range. Entry-level properties in the submarket — smaller original structures on larger lots — trade in the $800,000 to $1.2 million range and attract both owner-occupants and buyers looking for teardown or renovation opportunities. Mid-range properties with updated finishes and functional floor plans trade in the $1.2 million to $2 million range. Larger custom homes and new construction on premium lots carry prices from $2 million to $5 million and above.

The two price points that see the most activity in 75229 right now are the entry-level renovation opportunity segment and the $1.5 million to $2.5 million move-in ready segment, where buyers can get meaningful square footage and lot size without competing at the top of the Preston Hollow estate market.

Why buyers choose 75229

The location logic is straightforward. 75229 is within a short drive from downtown Dallas, close to the city's top schools, shopping districts, and major business centers. Specifically, buyers value the proximity to NorthPark Center, the Dallas Medical District, the Love Field corridor, and the major private schools clustered in the Park Cities and Preston Hollow area. For buyers who work in or near downtown Dallas, 75229 offers a residential setting with genuine lot size and architectural character at a price point below Highland Park and University Park.

The school district picture requires attention. Dallas ISD covers the majority of Preston Hollow, and DISD school quality varies enormously by campus. Buyers with school-age children who are targeting 75229 need to understand which specific schools serve their address — both the assigned public schools and the private school options in the area — before making a decision. This is an area where local market knowledge matters significantly. The school assignment for a property two blocks away can be different from yours, and that difference affects both the living experience and the resale value.

What the buyer pool looks like

The buyer profile for 75229 in 2026 is more diverse than a single luxury market label suggests. Established Dallas professionals and executives who want to be inside the city rather than in the northern suburbs make up a significant segment. Corporate relocatees coming to Dallas for positions in the medical district or downtown financial sector are another consistent buyer type — 75229 offers a lifestyle and location that appeals to buyers coming from similar established urban neighborhoods in other cities. And a growing segment of value-oriented luxury buyers who cannot or choose not to compete at Park Cities prices are finding 75229 an attractive alternative with better lot size and more architectural variety.

What to watch for when buying

Lot configuration matters more than square footage in this submarket. A smaller home on a 15,000 square foot lot in the right location carries more long-term value than a larger home on a constrained lot, both for lifestyle and for future optionality. Many 75229 buyers who are not planning to build or renovate immediately are still buying with an eye toward what the lot supports.

Condition and renovation scope require honest assessment. The older housing stock in 75229 includes properties with systems that have not been updated in decades. A professional inspection with specific attention to foundation, roofing, plumbing, and electrical is essential. The price gap between a turnkey property and one that requires significant work can look attractive until the renovation costs are accurately scoped.

The bottom line

Dallas 75229 is a market that rewards buyers who understand the nuances of the submarket — lot size, school assignments, condition relative to price, and how the different pockets within the ZIP code compare. Generic luxury market research will not give you an accurate picture. Local knowledge and current comparable data will.
