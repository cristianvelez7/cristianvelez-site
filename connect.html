About

Local. Direct. Experienced.

I grew up in Plano and McKinney, so I know DFW streets, schools, and submarkets firsthand, not from a spreadsheet. Beyond representing buyers and sellers, I invest in real estate myself, which means I understand numbers, risk, and deal structure the way an investor does. I negotiate hard for my clients and track results, not just transactions closed. I give straightforward guidance: what a property is really worth, what to fix, and what to walk away from. I am licensed with Corey Simpson & Associates and serve as principal of Elite Offer Group.
